Development process · 2022 – 2026

Two weeks in 2022. Then 1,333 days of nothing.

Block Shooter started as a two-week bootcamp project: click the squares before they reach the middle. I finished it, and did not open it again for three and a half years. This is what happened when I did: seventeen milestones across four years, every one checked out of git, run in a browser and recorded.

How this was made

Block Shooter is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies, which is the only reason a commit from 2022 still runs today. Each milestone was checked out into its own working copy, served, played and recorded. All seventeen still run.

One of them did not. The first attempt at the scrolling camera threw on an undefined constant and took the game loop with it; the commit captured here is the fix that followed. Play the current build at lrakoto.github.io/blockshooter, or go back to the rest of my work.
