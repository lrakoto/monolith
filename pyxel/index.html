How this was made

The game began in June 2026 as a three.js scene: pixel sprites lit by real 3D lights, run through a post-processing stack. In September it was rebuilt from nothing as a 2D canvas renderer with no runtime dependencies at all: every light, reflection, bloom and lens flare hand-written. The rebuild looks better than the engine did, which was not the expected result.

The captures were made by checking out each milestone commit into its own build, installing its original dependencies, running it in a browser and recording it. All eighteen still compile. Play the current build at lrakoto.github.io/Pyxel2, or go back to the rest of my work.
